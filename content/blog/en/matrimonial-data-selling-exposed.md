A few days after opening a profile on a marriage site, suddenly calls start coming from unknown numbers? Random messages, strange offers? Many people assume it's a coincidence. But the truth is, a big part of the income of most mainstream matrimonial sites comes from selling your personal information.

Of course, they don't call it "selling data." They give it a nice name, like "Pro Membership" or "Contact Unlock Package". In other words, anyone who pays a small amount for a pro subscription gets your name, family address, phone number, and photo in their hands.

## 1. What is really going on behind 'Pro Membership'?
Look closely at the business model of these platforms and the picture becomes clear:

- **Pay, and the information is yours:** The site doesn't check whether the person paying is fit to be your life partner, or whether their background is verified. The moment money comes in, your sensitive details open up on their screen.
- **Photo downloads and screenshots:** Once a profile is unlocked, full-resolution photos can be downloaded or screenshotted. Where that photo spreads afterward, nobody knows.
- **Sold to third parties:** Numbers collected from this pro database are put into lists and sold to call centers, insurance companies, and marketing agencies.

**Put simply:** there, you are not the user. Your life story is the product they sell.
