Finding a life partner online requires patience, open communication, and above all, personal safety. While Match Media enforces verification checks, your active vigilance ensures a completely secure matchmaking journey.

## 1. Protect Your Direct Contact Information
Never share your private phone number, WhatsApp, home address, or personal social media handles during initial conversations. Communicate exclusively through the platform's secure messaging system until mutual comfort and trust are established.

## 2. Prioritize Verified Member Profiles
Look for the green **Verified Member** badge. These profiles have undergone strict identity and document verification. If a profile lacks verification, gently request them to complete the process before moving discussions forward.

## 3. Involve Family and Guardians Early
Online matrimony in Bangladesh is deeply tied to family trust. Before planning any private conversations or meetings, request a mutual phone call between family guardians to confirm background details.

## 4. Choose Safe, Public Spaces for First Meetups
When both families agree to an initial personal meeting, always choose a well-lit, public cafe or restaurant. Always inform a trusted friend or family member about the meetup location and time.

## 5. Watch for Financial Red Flags
Match Media will never ask you to transfer money to members for gifts, travel expenses, or emergency fees. Anyone demanding monetary assistance or business favors should be reported immediately.
