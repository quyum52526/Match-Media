On Match Media, a viewing request gives the profile owner a choice about when to reveal a protected photo. Take time to review a profile and request access only when you are ready.

A photo request is not permission to exchange contact details. Share those only when both people agree. Until then, use the in-app communication tools and respect a declined or unanswered request.
