The moment marriage comes up at home, it starts. "Look for a good groom." "Anyone suitable for her?" The word reaches the ghotok, and with it goes a photo and a biodata. And that's exactly where the real trouble begins.

Once you hand over a photo, you don't know where it goes or whose phone it lands on. Plenty of families find out later that their daughter's picture is sitting in some Facebook group. Nobody even asked.

### 1. What can happen when a ghotok handles your photo and details
None of this is new in our country, whether it's traditional matchmaking or the countless marriage groups online:
- **No record at all:** How many phones saved the photo? Who forwarded it? You'll never know.
- **Leaks to public groups:** Photos, height, age, even mobile numbers get posted on Facebook pages and groups. Nobody thinks to ask permission.
- **Calls from strangers:** Once your number is out, the calls and messages don't stop. Scammers can find you too.
- **Photo misuse:** A photo that's openly available online can easily become a fake profile or a fraud.

It's not only ghotoks. Many matrimonial sites also hand your details to strangers for money. Read more: [Biodata for Sale Under the Name of "Pro Membership"](/en/blog/matrimonial-data-selling-exposed).

### 2. How Match Media stops this
Match Media is not just another biodata directory. It was built from day one as a [Privacy-First Matrimonial Platform](/en). The control over your information stays with you.
- **A. Server-Side Irreversible Photo Blur:** Every photo you upload is blurred by default. And this isn't a simple CSS blur that anyone can undo with Inspect Element. The blur is created on the server by reducing the original photo's resolution. Without your permission, the original photo never reaches another person's device.
